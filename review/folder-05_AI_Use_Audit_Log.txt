Modernising Item Development with AI and LLMs
5. AI-Use Audit Log
A transparent record of what AI contributed, what humans changed, why decisions were made, and what still requires evidence.
Purpose
Use this log when AI or LLMs contribute to item drafting, review, refinement, variation, or screening. The log supports accountability and reproducibility by distinguishing AI output from human decisions. It should be adapted to organizational policy, security classification, and data governance requirements.
Core principles
Principle
Operational meaning
Traceability
Record enough information to reconstruct what the AI was asked to do and how its output influenced the item.
Human accountability
Name the human role responsible for accepting, rejecting, or revising substantive recommendations.
Data minimization
Do not record or transmit personal, confidential, or secure content beyond what policy permits.
Evidence separation
Label AI judgments about difficulty, bias, or validity as hypotheses or screening observations unless supported by independent evidence.
Version control
Link the log entry to the specific item version reviewed or generated.
Proportionality
Record substantive decisions. Minor spelling or formatting changes do not need the same detail as changes to the construct, key, scoring, or fairness.

Workshop exercise
During the lab
Choose one AI recommendation that you accepted, one that you edited, and one that you rejected. Record each decision in Section C. Your goal is not to agree with the model. Your goal is to make the reasoning behind the human decision visible and defensible.
