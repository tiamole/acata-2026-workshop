Minimal Worked Example Set for Participants
3. Item Review Rubric: Minimal Worked Example
Illustrative scenario: Secondary level science, applying Ohm's law. This example is for workshop practice only and is not tied to a specific examination board or national curriculum.
Item being reviewed
A simple circuit has a voltage of 12 V across a resistor of 4 ohms. What is the current in the circuit? A. 0.33 A  B. 3 A  C. 8 A  D. 48 A
Intended outcome: Given voltage and resistance, calculate current using I = V/R. Intended cognitive demand: Apply.
