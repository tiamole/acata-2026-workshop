Modernising Item Development with AI and LLMs
1. Item Blueprint Mini-Template
A practical planning tool for defining what an item should measure before AI is used to draft it.
Purpose
Use this template before item generation. A blueprint gives the human team control over the construct, content boundaries, cognitive demand, response format, and coverage. AI should receive these decisions as constraints rather than inventing them.
How to use this template
Define the assessment context and learner population.
State the intended learning outcome or competency in observable terms.
Describe the evidence that would demonstrate the competency.
Select the intended cognitive process and item format.
Specify content that is in scope, out of scope, and required for fairness.
Use the completed blueprint as the input for item generation and later item review.
Important: A blueprint is a design specification. It does not replace content validation, piloting, item analysis, or psychometric calibration.

G. Fairness and accessibility constraints
Use only context that is necessary to measure the target construct.
Avoid unnecessary dependence on regional expressions, uncommon names, specialist vocabulary, socioeconomic assumptions, or culture-specific experiences unless they are part of the construct.
Separate reading complexity from subject difficulty when reading is not the construct.
Ensure diagrams, tables, symbols, units, and instructions are interpretable by the intended population and compatible with required accommodations.
Do not assume that an AI fairness check demonstrates absence of bias. Empirical fairness analyses, including differential item functioning when appropriate, require response data.
