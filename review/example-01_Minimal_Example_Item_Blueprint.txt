Minimal Worked Example Set for Participants
1. Item Blueprint Mini Template: Minimal Worked Example
Illustrative scenario: Secondary level science, applying Ohm's law. This example is for workshop practice only and is not tied to a specific examination board or national curriculum.
How to use this example
Participants should use this as a model for the amount and type of information expected. Replace the example content with the construct, outcome, learner population, and item type relevant to your own assessment context.
A. Assessment context
Field
Minimal example
Assessment purpose
Low stakes formative check after instruction.
Target learners
Secondary level science learners.
Domain
Basic electricity.
Item format
Four option selected response item.
Administration
Computer based or paper based.
Intended use of score
Identify whether learners can apply a basic quantitative relationship before moving to more complex circuit problems.
B. Construct and learning outcome
Field
Minimal example
Construct
Application of the relationship among voltage, current, and resistance in a simple direct current circuit.
Learning outcome
Given voltage and resistance values for a simple circuit, the learner will calculate current using I = V/R.
Evidence of learning
The learner selects the current value obtained by correctly dividing voltage by resistance.
Content boundaries
Single resistor, direct current, numerical values with simple arithmetic. No series or parallel networks.
Cognitive demand
Apply a known relationship to a straightforward situation.
C. Item design specification
Field
Minimal example
Stem requirement
Present only the information needed to solve the problem.
Options
One correct answer and three plausible distractors.
Correct answer basis
12 V divided by 4 ohms equals 3 A.
Distractor logic
Include errors that reflect plausible misconceptions, such as multiplication instead of division or confusing quantities.
Language constraint
Use short sentences and define symbols through the context.
Fairness constraint
Avoid unnecessary cultural, socioeconomic, or specialized contextual knowledge.
Accessibility consideration
Do not require a diagram if the item can validly assess the same skill in text only.

E. Worked example item
A simple circuit has a voltage of 12 V across a resistor of 4 ohms. What is the current in the circuit?
A. 0.33 A
B. 3 A
C. 8 A
D. 48 A
Key: B. Rationale: I = V/R = 12/4 = 3 A.
