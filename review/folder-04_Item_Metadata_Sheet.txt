4. Item Metadata Sheet
A standardized record for item identity, construct alignment, provisional difficulty, scoring, distractor logic, accessibility, and lifecycle status.
Purpose
Complete one metadata record for every item that progresses beyond initial drafting. Consistent metadata makes item banks searchable, supports review, reduces version confusion, and prepares items for later psychometric analysis. Fields marked as provisional must be updated after pilot or operational data become available.
